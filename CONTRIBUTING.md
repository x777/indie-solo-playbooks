# Contributing

Russian files in `playbooks/` are the source of truth. `en/playbooks/` is a mirror of the same facts — do not add a claim that is missing from the Russian file.

Keep the skeleton: who and numbers, philosophy, launch, marketing, mistakes, 30 days, sources.

Do not invent quotes. Revenue figures are the founder's public claims, not audits.

If you add a playbook, add the English twin, a language switcher in both headers, a row in `README.md` and `en/README.md`, and a pointer from `AGENTS.md` plus the matching skill. Do not blend two founders into one method.
