## Philosophy

- “Market in 30 days or die.” If you cannot find a format that earns views in a month, the product will not die from code — it will die from silence.
- Distribution first. The free guide is itself a funnel into Post Bridge.
- Organic > ads. Do not buy views until you can make a video that holds watch time.
- Content-market fit before scaling. One winning format beats a hundred random clips.
- Do not look like an ad. Useful content about the pain; the product is the fix at the end or only in the caption.
- A new account is not a dead account. Zero views usually mean you posted before warmup.
- In 2026: an agent can post for you across networks, but the brand is still built by repeating one name.

## Launch system (official 30-day timeline)

### Day 1 — new accounts

1. Create a **new** TikTok and a **new** Instagram. An old account with the wrong history is worse for the algorithm than a clean one.
2. Do not look like a bot: one account at a time, preferably different emails.
3. A bio that moves traffic: what it is, why, where to go. New TikTok accounts have no link — write the URL or app name in text. On Instagram — business/creator and a link in bio.

### Days 2–8 — warmup (DO NOT POST)

Skip this and the algorithm buries the new account in 0-view jail.

- 7 days is a safe minimum. 3 days sometimes works; sometimes you have to go back to warmup.
- 15 minutes of niche scrolling on each platform. Comment, reply, follow relevant accounts.
- Be a person, not a robot.
- Save clips with 100k+ views or 1k+ likes that you can remake for your niche. 3–5 per day from day 4.
- **No posts** until warmup ends.

### Days 8–30 — content-market fit

- 1 video a day on the warmed account. Not 4–6 “just to post.”
- Take a saved viral clip: same hook and format, same sound, different context for your product.
- CTA in the caption and a pinned comment, not a hard sell in the first seconds.
- Main metrics at this stage: watch time and comments.
- Do not chase “100 formats.” You are looking for one that repeats.

### After day 30 — scale only when

Do not touch a second account until you have:

- 30 days of posting in a row;
- at least one video at 100k+ or several at 10k+.

His scaling math:

- 1 account × 1 post/day → ~100 downloads/day
- 2 accounts × 3 posts/day → ~600 downloads/day
- 4 accounts on TikTok + Instagram → 30,000+ downloads in 30 days

Scaling rules:

- every new account gets another 7-day warmup;
- the first 10 posts are **only from the app, by hand**;
- do not automate an account until clips hold 500+ views;
- do not post the same file to the same platform twice unless you changed metadata / sound or months have passed.

Phases: post more often on the main account first (days 30–45), then a second account on the same platform with a full warmup, then other networks (60+).

## Marketing and distribution

1. **Short video as the main channel.** TikTok → Reels → Shorts. One clip, several platforms — after the account is already alive.
2. **Trends are a boost, not a strategy.** A sound / format with 10k–50k uses. Adapt to the niche in 24–48 hours. Do not replace a working format with a trend. Case: “chill guy” for Curiosity Quench — 100k+ per clip, one at 980k / 73k likes.
3. **View → customer funnel.** Video → profile → bio → landing → download / pay. Optimize every step, not only the hook.
4. **Show the product, do not narrate it.** Screenshot, before/after, the app in the background with no “buy” line.
5. **Free tools and the guide** as top-of-funnel (Growth Guide, Instagram grid/carousel tools, username checkers).
6. **SEO alternative pages:** Buffer, Hootsuite, Later, Publer, Postiz.
7. **2026, agents.** MCP: “the video is on my laptop — post it to TikTok, Reels and Shorts tomorrow at 9.” The file is handed to the agent via upload in Post Bridge. Do not confuse this with the other point: he also writes that clicks from other people’s platforms often do not convert; branded search converts.
8. **Ship or Die community / Marc Lou’s circle.** Distribution inside indie hackers, not only cold Reels traffic.

## Monetization

- First it was consumer traffic into an app (downloads → payment).
- Then the tool he used to post — Post Bridge (scheduler + studio + API/MCP).
- Free layer: the guide and small tools with no signup. Paid — unlimited posts, multi-account, agents.
- Do not expect one viral clip to pay the bill. People buy after they have seen the name several times.

## Mistakes he names

- Posting on the day you create the account.
- Shipping 5 videos a day without a format that already holds watch time.
- Hard-selling in the first seconds (the algorithm treats that as an ad).
- Counting views as money without a bio, a link, and a planted CTA.
- Autoposting from day one and the same file on one platform.
- Scaling accounts before the first hit.
- Chasing clicks from other platforms while the brand does not rank for its own name.

---

## 30 days in the Jack Friks style

1. Day 1: new TikTok + Instagram, bio with the offer and the path to the product.
2. Days 2–8: warmup only. Save 20–30 references to remake.
3. Days 8–30: one deliberate video a day. Copy hook and format from niche hits; do not invent a “unique” script from scratch.
4. CTA in the caption + pinned comment. In the video — the pain and a demo, not a pitch.
5. By day 30: if there is a hit — a second account with the same warmup. If not — change the format, do not add product features.
