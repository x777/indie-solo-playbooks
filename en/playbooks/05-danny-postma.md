## Who this is and the numbers he publishes

Dutch solo founder. Company is Singapore-based. One of the first to put GPT-3 into production.

Official HeadshotPro about page, September 2026: 17,000,000 generated headshots; 200,000+ sessions; $10,000,000+ revenue since launch; Fortune 500 teams, including HubSpot sales.

From his history and write-ups: Headlime ~$1k MRR Dec 2020 → ~$20k by Feb 2021 → sold into the Jasper orbit for a seven-figure sum; ProfilePicture.AI shipped in ~30 hours after Stable Diffusion; HeadshotPro 16 Mar 2023 write-ups cited ~$100k in 14 days and ~$300k/month at peak (not an about-page number); 20% affiliate with reports of payouts >$50k/month; later bought adjacent AI products (Hesti AI, Dreamchanted) and 301'd them.

## Philosophy

- Sell the result, not the model. People need a LinkedIn / team photo, not an AI image generator.
- Boring on purpose. Deep Agency got press. HeadshotPro got money.
- Commercial search beats virality.
- Find a channel first, then squeeze it. March 2025: “Your first marketing goal is to figure out what channel works for you. Your second goal is to maximise the hell out of that channel.”
- Talk about the product often.
- An exit is a normal outcome.
- Quality catches up over years. Money arrived before perfect quality.

## Launch system

### HeadshotPro, March 2023

1. Narrow offer: selfie → corporate photo in a few hours.
2. Domain matched the query: headshotpro.com.
3. Landing is before/after, not a feature list.
4. Price from day one: packs like $29 / $39 / $59.
5. X thread 16 Mar 2023: pain → examples → 5 steps → 24-hour discount.
6. Product Hunt is a backlink machine, not the main sales channel.

July 2026: rebuilt the site through a SKILL.md pipeline. September 2026: AI writes code well and landing pages badly.

---

## Marketing and distribution

1. Programmatic SEO by city and role. 200+ pages for “professional headshots [city]”. Filter: KD < 20–25 with volume. First traffic ~3 months, normal ~6.
2. A blog aimed at the same commercial query.
3. Product Hunt as a backlink (DR ~90), even last place.
4. ~20% affiliate. Second engine after search.
5. X at the start. Then money from Google.
6. Ads once unit economics work.
7. Local currency on the price tag ($29 as €29).

---

## Monetization

- A pack per session, not unlimited generations.
- Margin holds while compute per order is far cheaper than the check.
- Affiliates are part of CAC.

---

## Mistakes

- A generic AI image generator and waiting for virality.
- Confusing press with money.
- Treating Product Hunt as a sales channel.
- Expecting SEO next week.
- Not squeezing a channel that already works.
- One-shotting a landing page through AI.

---

## 30 days in Danny Postma's style

1. Find a query people already pay for. Check volume and KD.
2. 20 before/after examples.
3. Landing + payment in a week. The domain should say what it is.
4. X thread: pain → before/after → price. PH for the link.
5. 10–20 tail pages by city or role.
6. If there are payments — affiliates. Ads only when the check and delivery cost are visible.

SEO will not hit in 30 days. In 30 days you install the system that starts paying a quarter later.
