## Philosophy

- **Sell the result, not the model.** People need a photo for LinkedIn / a team, not an “AI image generator.”
- **Boring on purpose.** Deep Agency got press. HeadshotPro got money.
- **Commercial search beats virality.** A competitor goes viral — demand in Google grows — the one already sitting on the keyword gets paid.
- **Find a channel first, then squeeze it.** March 2025: “Your first marketing goal is to figure out what channel works for you. Your second goal is to maximise the hell out of that channel.”
- **Talk about the product often.** People hate selling so much that an ordinary account already separates you from 99%.
- **An exit is a normal outcome.** He sold Headlime and built the next hit.
- **Quality catches up over years.** Official frame: POC 2022 → launch 2023 → “good enough for work” in 2025. Money arrived before “perfect” quality.

## Launch system

### HeadshotPro, March 2023

1. Narrow offer: selfie → corporate photo in a few hours.
2. Domain matched the query: headshotpro.com.
3. Landing is before/after, not a feature list.
4. Price from day one: packs like $29 / $39 / $59.
5. X thread 16 Mar 2023: pain (a team photo at up to $500/person) → examples → 5 steps → 24-hour discount.
6. Product Hunt is a backlink machine, not the main sales channel.

From the early launches: a lifetime deal at the start brings live money; copy matters more than design; let people try without an email at the door.

In July 2026 he rebuilt the site through a SKILL.md pipeline (copy → voice → structure → wireframes → SEO audit). Not a one-shot. In September 2026 he wrote separately: AI writes code well and landing pages badly — that is why the chain of steps.

## Marketing and distribution

1. **Programmatic SEO by city and role.** 200+ pages for “professional headshots [city]”, plus realtor / lawyer / LinkedIn. Do not fight “headshots” head-on. Filter in the write-ups: KD < 20–25 with volume. First traffic ~3 months, normal ~6.
2. **A blog aimed at the same commercial query.** Not “what is AI,” but pages a person with a card is already going to.
3. **Product Hunt as a backlink.** Even last place is a win: a link from DR ~90 and a pile of reposts. That is enough for a tail with KD < 10.
4. **~20% affiliate.** Creators bring both sales and links. Second engine after search.
5. **X at the start.** The thread collects the first wave. Then money comes from Google.
6. **Ads once unit economics work.** In August 2026 he writes that advertising is a normal tactic. Not the first channel.
7. **Local currency on the price tag.** $29 as €29 for a European, in his telling, lifted conversion.

## Monetization

- A pack per photoshoot, not “unlimited generations.” The check is higher than a toy avatar because it replaces a studio at $200–500.
- Margin holds while compute per order is an order of magnitude cheaper than the check.
- Affiliates are part of CAC, without a marketing office.
- When volume no longer fits in one pair of hands — a thin team. The GPU bill does not care about a personal brand.

---

## Mistakes that follow from his history

- A generic “AI image generator” and waiting for a viral. Novelty cools, search does not.
- Confusing press with money.
- Treating Product Hunt as a sales channel, not a DR source.
- Expecting SEO next week. He himself: 3 months to a flow, 6 to normal.
- Not squeezing a channel that already works.
- One-shotting a landing page through AI without a chain of steps.

---

## 30 days in Danny Postma's style

1. Find a query people already pay for (not “AI for everyone”). Check volume and KD.
2. 20 before/after examples. That is both the offer and the content.
3. Landing + payment in a week. The domain name should say what it is.
4. X thread: pain → before/after → price. PH for the link.
5. 10–20 tail pages by city or role. Not 200 on day one.
6. If there are payments — affiliates. Ads only when the check and the delivery cost are visible.

SEO will not hit in 30 days. In 30 days you install the system that starts paying a quarter later.
