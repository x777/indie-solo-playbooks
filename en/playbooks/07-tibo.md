## Philosophy 2026

Old way: *build it and they will come.*  
New way: *build distribution, then build the product.*

- AI made building cheap. The bottleneck is distribution.
- Do not invent a market. June 2026: “$1m MRR without inventing a single original idea.” A crowd = demand. The game is your angle.
- You do not need 10,000 followers. Tweet Hunter started with no audience.
- Change the pitch, not the product.
- 70% of $10k is better than 100% of $1k. A share for a distribution partner is leverage.
- Starting goal: 100 people at $50/mo = $5k MRR. Not viral.
- Talk to people or fail. Most skip manual delivery and DMs.
- If you are not growing in one channel — there is no PMF.

## Launch system (“if I had to start over”)

### Week 1 — do not build

- Days 1–3: a raw idea on X/LinkedIn. No landing, no waitlist. “Thinking of making X for Y.”
- Days 4–7: who wrote “I need this” unprompted.
- Rule: fewer than 10 unprompted replies — kill it. That is how he killed 5+ ideas.

The main mistake: skip week 1 and start coding.

### Months 1–2 — a skeleton

- One workflow. No dashboard, no settings. *Ugly is fine. Broken is not.*
- 5–10 people from week 1 — free, “break it and tell me what is missing.”
- If they do not use it twice — it is not a product yet.

### Months 3–4 — noise, not a “big launch”

Goal: $1k MRR. $200–300 by month 4 is still fine.

- A post every day: even $47 numbers, the backstage.
- 10 personal DMs a day. Template: observation → one-line pitch → a light ask.
- Anti-launch: do not spend three months prepping PH. After that — monthly mini-launches of features.

### Months 5–8 — three channels, then one

At $1k–5k MRR run 60 days:

1. SEO — 2–3 long-tail articles a week
2. Ads — $300–500/mo, kill if CAC > LTV
3. Affiliates — 30%, templates that are “idiotically simple”

Keep **one** channel.

### Months 9–12 — squeeze what works

- SEO → write daily
- Affiliates → commission to 40%, turn top ones into partners
- Social → 2 posts/day, creator equity 0.1%

## Marketing in practice

1. **The product lives where the customer lives.** A tool for X — on X.
2. **Equity for a distribution partner.** JK Molina ~25% of Tweet Hunter. Maker + distributor beats 100% of a small pie.
3. **Micro-shares to 15 accounts** at 0.1%. Every feature mini-launch went through 15 mouths.
4. **Affiliates after a proven product.** July 2026: Outrank 30%, Revid 20% (compute is expensive), SuperX 40%.
5. **Free neighbor tools** on PH — traffic and links, not “the main launch.”
6. **Reddit is a separate market.** Bazzly is exactly that: months you cannot show the product.
7. **PH is not dead, but it is not enough.**

## Price and monetization

- B2B SaaS: $40–100/mo. $99 beats $9: less support.
- A trial with a card. Free users lie to themselves.
- Do not rebuild pricing every month before $100k MRR.
- New projects sit under the TMaker holding; a separate legal entity when they hit $100k MRR.

## Mistakes

- Skip validation and start building.
- Run every channel at once (he did this in 2020).
- Optimize features, not distribution.
- Wait for 10k followers before the first sale.
- Keep 100% equity and sit on $3k when a partner can deliver $20k.
- Hunt a “new market” instead of an angle in a crowd.
- Treat free users as validation.

---

## 30 days in Tibo’s style

**Week 1.** The idea on X with no landing. Fewer than 10 “I need this” — next idea.

**Weeks 2–3.** One workflow. 5–10 people from the comments — into DMs. They must come back.

**Week 4.** Anti-launch: a price on the site, a thread, 10 DMs a day. Do not wait for Product Hunt.

After that: one channel for 60 days. If someone has the ideal customer’s audience — give equity, do not pay for a one-off promo post.
