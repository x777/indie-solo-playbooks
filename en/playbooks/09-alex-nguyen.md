## Philosophy

- Not features — users. Then paying users. You do not need a million users: 2,000 people at $5/mo = $10k MRR.
- Profit > pretty MRR. $10k/mo with fat margin beats $100k at 9%. Revenue shows on a screenshot; profit does not.
- Attention > product. Distribution is the 2026 moat.
- Consistency > virality. 100 posts beat one “lucky” clip. But one real viral can close $10k.
- Weekly is often better than monthly for B2C: people take it fast and leave fast. 10–30% margin for B2C is already normal. The main line item is marketing.
- $5 live validates harder than 5,000 free users.
- iOS and web first: conversion is higher than Android.

## Launch system

### His B2C playbook

1. An MVP in weeks, not months. Ugly, but usable. Paywall + RevenueCat on day 6–7.
2. One channel. TikTok, X, or Reddit — dominate one, do not smear.
3. Ship → measure → fix. A weekly cycle.
4. Monetize immediately.
5. Retention beats growth hacks.
6. Organic first, then ads. Ads amplify what works. They do not find the offer.

### 2026 playbook toward an exit

1. SensorTower: find apps at $1M+/mo (real revenue is often ×1.5–2).
2. Build it better. Not perfect — better.
3. Distribute on TikTok, IG, YouTube, X.
4. Organic → creators + ads.
5. Sell to the same large players you watched. Or repeat the cycle.

The feature itself should be a viral loop: one screen — and the clip assembles itself.

## Marketing: an organic farm

### TikTok as a machine

His scheme that reached $17.5k/mo with no ads:

- 2–3 clips a day per account. No skipped days.
- 5 phones (iPhone SE 2020 / iPhone 8 Plus). Max 3 accounts per phone.
- One account is the official product. The rest are “KOL” reviews of the same app.
- VPN + many accounts on one device → often 100–1,000 views. TikTok reads that as spam.
- He films them himself: a screen, a process, one feature, a strong hook. No reuploads.
- Duplicate to Lemon8 and Threads. He had a 700k post on Lemon8.

0 views in the first hours on a new account is normal. 0 after 12–24 hours is an account / reupload / VPN / pace problem. Do not spam further.

### ASO

The first App Store image is a preview video. Impressions grew ×3, then ×6 for him.

### Spend stages

- $0 → $20k: post yourself every day. Build an AI content engine.
- $20k → $100k: hire UGC creators. Do not drop personal content.
- $100k+: turn on ads on a format that already works.

Every post: hook + value + CTA. A Gen Z niche where the pain is already in trends: ADHD, sleep, mental health, fitness.

## Product and price

- Mobile is more expensive to acquire and loses payers faster than web. The store takes 15–30%. He likes building it, but he counts margin honestly.
- Do not wait to be “ready for a million users.” One clip, one creator, one clear onboarding is enough.
- Weird ideas with a strong pain can make money. SensorTower often under-reports revenue.

---

## Mistakes

- Waiting for a perfect app before the first clip.
- Pouring ads before organic has shown a format.
- A VPN and 10 accounts on one phone.
- Spamming posts when views are zero.
- Chasing MRR and ignoring margin and the store fee.
- Telling everything on X and getting a 1:1 copy overnight. He has already seen that.

---

## 30 days in Alex Nguyen’s style

1. Day 1: the app in the store + a paywall + a preview video as the first screenshot.
2. Two phones, no more than 3 accounts per device. One is the product, the rest are reviews.
3. Every day 2 clips per account: hook + feature + CTA. No zero days.
4. Duplicate working formats to IG / Threads / X.
5. Watch payments, not views. There is $5 — double the format. There is not — another hook or another pain, not ten features.
