## Philosophy

- **Sell the outcome, not “another Buffer.”** SaaS is not dead. You just cannot sell it as software: anyone can ship a clone.
- **Take a validated category.** Schedulers have existed for 20 years. The angle is agents / self-host / n8n / MCP, not “yet another Buffer.”
- **Volume > polish.** 30 UGC pieces for one hit at 200k+.
- **Viral is money and a hack, not luck.** A person has to see you many times.
- **Same input → exponential output.** 10 September: “I can leave for a month — Postiz will still grow $20k MRR.” Other people’s problem is build / kill / build.
- **Nobody cares about your features, they care about the moment.**

## Launch system

1. **Public repo as the first channel.** r/selfhosted gave 20k–500k on each release. Awesome-lists, Coolify, Unraid, Docker.
2. **Product Hunt with outreach, not “ship and wait.”** Several launches; the first was #1 of the day/week/month. Votes do not appear without an audience.
3. **Free tools and directories.** 19 channels × 9 tools, backlinks, Outrank.
4. **Ride someone else’s wave. Do not try to become the wave.** n8n, MCP, then agents / OpenClaw. API + CLI + skill — so the product lives without the dashboard.
5. **Public Stripe and daily MRR.** That is both content and proof.

## Marketing 2026 (three tactics from the article)

### 1. X: video → article → quote all week

- Monday: a “how to make money” video. Postiz is in frame, but it is not an ad.
- All week: reply and quote-post the same video / cuts.
- Next week: transcript through Astra → X Article in his voice. Obsession over title and cover. The article also ranks in Google because x.com is a strong domain.
- Numbers from the write-up: video 84k → article 204k; hits 200k–7M.

### 2. UGC with no product in frame

- Hook + outcome + how-to + CTA (“type SCHEDULE”).
- One clip a day, only the hook changes. 200–500 views until one does 200k+. Usually ~30 pieces.
- Scale: a creator at $20–45 per clip, or many pages + an agent.
- A page that only posts and never sits in the feed dies. Live engagement is required. A Meta ban is a working risk — he writes that plainly.

### 3. TikTok slideshows and AEO

- Slideshow with no product in frame. Example from the article — 234k.
- Plant human answers where ChatGPT looks for the category (real Reddit, not bot spam). Next — hire for AEO.

What he adds on the $2M → $3M path: more UGC / AI-UGC, more lead-magnet accounts, AEO, 10x X Articles, and paid reposts.

## Mistakes

- Selling “better than Buffer” in a market of thousands of clones.
- Waiting for one post to go viral and not quoting it for a week.
- Making 3 UGC pieces and declaring the format dead.
- Killing pages by only posting and never sitting in the feed.
- Jumping between products when you need to keep one input for years.

---

## 30 days in Nevo’s style

1. A category people already buy + an angle (agent / self-host / API).
2. A public metric every day.
3. One week: a useful video on X + replies + quotes. Next week — an X Article from that video.
4. 30 raw UGC pieces with different hooks, not 3 “pretty” ones.
5. One human answer in a thread LLMs already visit for your category.
