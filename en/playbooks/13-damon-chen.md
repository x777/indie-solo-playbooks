## Philosophy

- **Start with a query people already search.** testimonial.to ranks for “testimonial”, pdf.ai for “pdf ai”. The domain is the channel.
- **X is the start and the flywheel. Google is the scale.** Early on, 80–90% of customers came from Twitter. Then SEO overtook it.
- **Flywheel: ship → old customers get happier → new ones see it.** “Build in public on Twitter, kinda like flywheel marketing.”
- **The product advertises itself.** A Wall of Love with the Testimonial logo — a visitor on the client’s page becomes a lead.
- **A portfolio of neighboring utilities.** Not one hit forever: PDF.ai, PDFgen, free tools under the same queries.
- **SEO is not dead. You are just impatient.** Six months to traffic is normal.

## Launch system

1. **A pain you already feel.** Testimonial came from four products that could not collect social proof.
2. **Reuse old code.** ~80% of Indielog + a landing page + Stripe. MVP in one to three weeks.
3. **PH + LTD for validation, then subscription.** The $199 lifetime brought real money and feedback. He did not stay on LTD for long.
4. **Quit in public.** The $1k MRR goal was public — that is marketing too.
5. **A 6-month cycle:** ship → listen → a feature for people who already pay → back to X.

---

## Marketing and distribution

1. **Wall of Love.** The client embeds the widget → Testimonial logo → a new signup. A PLG loop.
2. **30% affiliate.** His own customers (agencies, creators) work better than cold wanderers. An income calculator on the program page. A CTA in onboarding.
3. **Exact-match and typos.** He bought testimonial.io for $35k — in two years $200k+ from those domains with no commission.
4. **A family of pages for neighboring queries.** Free PDF tools feed PDF.ai. Paid plan at the bottom, free limit at the top.
5. **X threads about features and numbers.** March 2022: a thread at 1,600 RT / 7,800 likes worked because context already existed.
6. **He dropped cold calls.** “One person, does not scale.” Instead: video → cuts into social + an article for SEO.

---

## Monetization

- LTD only at the start, then subscription. He raised the price twice in six months.
- Affiliates are part of CAC, not “giving the business away.”
- Free limit on top for PDF.ai, paid plan below. Free tools are the SEO door.
- Stripe retries: if the card fails 8 times and you do not fix it — “that is not MRR, that is hope” (September 2026).

---

## Mistakes

- Building a product with no way to collect social proof (the first four sides).
- Living on LTD and never moving to subscription.
- Waiting one month for SEO and abandoning the page.
- Cold calls instead of a product loop.
- Treating a viral thread as the beginning. He had a flywheel first.

---

## 30 days in Damon’s style

1. One fat query people already search. A domain for it if you can.
2. A utility with a result in 30 seconds. Free limit + pay below.
3. PH or an X thread. LTD only to get first money, then subscription immediately.
4. One money page + 5 neighbors (comparisons, a free tool, a typo).
5. If people pay — put your name in the widget. That is a channel, not a brand.
