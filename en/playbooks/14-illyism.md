## Philosophy

- SEO captures demand. It does not create it. You cannot validate an offer through Google.
- A channel for people willing to wait. A grid of pages feeds it, not one post.
- Indie is not a lottery. The system: free audits → consulting → SaaS.
- Authority in public. A free roast of Levelsio — 273k views — then they hired him.
- Outputmaxxing: the tool should emit a finished file (a deck, a link, a roast), not a “report.”
- SEO vs ads on the same $1,500 budget — ROI ×50 in favor of content (his case).

---

## Launch system

1. **Sell by hand first.** Free audit, roast, consulting on results. SEO is not on yet.
2. **Package it into a product** (SEORoast, LinkDR, GenPPT) once people already pay by hand.
3. **One money page + a cluster.** Clean title/H1, internal links.
4. **Update what already smells like traffic.** Do not write 50 new pages while the old ones do not hold a position.
5. **Watch other people’s articles** that already get traffic. GenPPT’s best month was not his blog.

## Marketing

### Layer 1 — free authority

SEO roast, teardown, a framework on X. Not “buy my course,” a public teardown of someone else’s site.

### Layer 2 — low-risk consulting

A call, pay for results. That is how he left a salary.

### Layer 3 — SEO grid

- A commercial page for money.
- 10 supporting pages: comparisons, “best X,” tools.
- Title and meta are their own job (he even has tools for this).
- Backlinks through LinkDR / manual outreach, not “hope for viral.”

### A product nobody searches for

His March 2026 playbook:

1. Write “top 20 best [category].”
2. Put your product at #1.
3. The other 19 are not competitors.
4. Email them: “I listed you, list us.”
5. Extra: open Sources in ChatGPT for your keyword and ask to be added to articles already cited.

Stop fighting for impossible keywords, build alliances.

## Mistakes

- Waiting on Google while the offer still does not sell by hand.
- Writing one big post and calling that SEO.
- Not tracking other people’s pages that already send traffic.
- Fighting for a keyword nobody searches instead of alliances and listicles.
- Building an “AI content detector” instead of emitting a finished result.

---

## 30 days in Ilias’s style

1. Not “grow on Google.” The offer already sells by hand or on X.
2. One public roast / teardown — that is both content and lead gen.
3. One commercial page + 10 supporting pages.
4. One “top 20” listicle and 19 emails to neighbors.
5. In parallel, keep the channel that already makes money. SEO comes second.
