# Paid ads

Read the matching playbook before writing a plan:

- Creatives are 80%, customer language, ads as an accelerator — `playbooks/06-nico-jeannen.md`
- Visual offer + paid after organic — `playbooks/05-danny-postma.md`

English mirrors: `en/playbooks/06-nico-jeannen.md`, `en/playbooks/05-danny-postma.md`.

No budget and no live sales — do not turn ads on. Switch to `indie-validate-offer` or `indie-launch-x`.

## Procedure

1. Does the offer already sell without ads? If not — stop.
2. Collect 5–10 customer phrases. The ad is built only from those.
3. One simple creative. Not a “pretty video.”
4. Count payback in months. An annual plan is often required for CAC to close.
5. Fix onboarding every day, not only the creative.

## Do not

- Do not turn ads on “to test the idea.”
- Do not mix ads with SEO and TikTok in the same 30 days unless there is a team.
- Do not write generic ads “for everyone.” If you do not have the customer's words, you do not have a creative.
