# Paid ads

Read the matching playbook before writing a plan. Russian canon; if the user writes in English, open the same filename under `en/playbooks/`:

- Creative is 80%, customer language, ads as an accelerator — `playbooks/06-nico-jeannen.md`
- Visual offer + paid after organic — `playbooks/05-danny-postma.md`

No budget and no live sales — do not turn ads on. Send them back to `indie-validate-offer` or `indie-launch-x`.

## Procedure

1. Does the offer already sell without ads? If not — stop.
2. Collect 5–10 customer phrases. Ad copy only from those.
3. One simple creative. Not a “beautiful video.”
4. Count payback in months. A yearly plan is often what makes CAC work.
5. Fix onboarding every day, not only the creative.

## Do not

- Do not turn ads on “to test the idea.”
- Do not mix ads with SEO and TikTok in the same 30 days unless they have a team.
