# SEO launch

Read first. Russian canon; if the user writes in English, open the same filename under `en/playbooks/`:

- Programmatic commercial pages, visual offer — `playbooks/05-danny-postma.md`
- Exact-match domains, free tools, Wall of Love — `playbooks/13-damon-chen.md`
- SEO captures demand, roast → consult → SaaS — `playbooks/14-illyism.md`

Default to 05 if the offer is visual or local (before/after, city/role pages). Default to 13 if the domain is the query. Default to 14 if nobody searches the product name.

## Procedure

1. Confirm the offer already sells by hand or on another channel. If not, switch to `indie-validate-offer`.
2. Pick one money query or exact-match domain. Not a new category.
3. One commercial page + a cluster of supporting pages or free tools.
4. If nobody searches the product name, use the listicle-alliance play from 14 instead of fighting an impossible keyword.
5. Track pages that already move, including other people's articles. Update those before writing 50 new posts.

## Do not

- Do not use Google to validate an offer that has never been sold.
- Do not promise rankings in 30 days. These founders treat SEO as a lagging channel.
