# Short-form launch

Read first:

- TikTok farm, weekly profit, not brand — `playbooks/09-alex-nguyen.md`
- Sell the outcome, 30 UGC for one hit, X article loop — `playbooks/11-nevo-david.md`

## Procedure

1. Sell an outcome in the first second. Do not open on the product UI.
2. Pick volume over polish. Plan ~30 raw pieces to buy one hit.
3. One hook family per week. Change the first line, keep the offer stable.
4. If they have X as well, use Nevo's video → article → quote-all-week loop. Do not run it as a side quest.
5. Judge the week by profit or trials, not average views.

## Do not

- Do not build a personal brand plan if they asked for a farm.
- Do not tell them to wait for one perfect video.
