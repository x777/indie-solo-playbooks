# Short-form launch

Read first. Russian canon; if the user writes in English, open the same filename under `en/playbooks/`:

- Audience-first warmup, launch as an event — `playbooks/01-jack-friks.md`
- TikTok farm, weekly profit, not brand — `playbooks/09-alex-nguyen.md`
- Sell the outcome, 30 UGC for one hit, X article loop — `playbooks/11-nevo-david.md`

Default to 01 if they need warmup before a launch. Default to 09 if they want a farm and weekly cash. Default to 11 if they already have a SaaS and need UGC volume.

## Procedure

1. Sell an outcome in the first second. Do not open on the product UI.
2. Pick volume over polish. Plan ~30 raw pieces to buy one hit — unless they chose 01, then warmup the audience before selling.
3. One hook family per week. Change the first line, keep the offer stable.
4. If they have X as well, use Nevo's video → article → quote-all-week loop. Do not run it as a side quest.
5. Judge the week by profit, trials, or warmup list growth — not average views.

## Do not

- Do not build a personal brand plan if they asked for a farm.
- Do not tell them to wait for one perfect video.
- Do not mix Jack's warmup calendar with Alex's farm in the same 30 days.
