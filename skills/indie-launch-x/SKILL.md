# X launch

Read the matching playbook before writing a plan:

- Ship or Die, one channel, portfolio — `playbooks/02-marc-lou.md`
- X as the engine for one product — `playbooks/04-tony-dinh.md`

Default to 02 if they want many small bets. Default to 04 if one product already exists.

Audience-first warmup on TikTok/IG belongs in `indie-launch-shortform` (playbook 01), not here. Paid Meta creatives belong in `indie-launch-ads` (05, 06).

## Procedure

1. Name the product in one line of outcome, not features.
2. Decide the single X job for 30 days: launch week or post-launch loop.
3. Pull the 30-day block from the chosen playbook and rewrite it with their URLs and dates.
4. Give them a posting cadence they can keep. Volume without a loop is noise.
5. Separate content that earns attention from content that sells. Do not sell in every post.

## Do not

- Do not send them to SEO or TikTok in the same 30 days unless they already have that channel working.
- Do not invent tweet templates that contradict the chosen playbook.
- Do not route Nico Jeannen or Danny Postma through this skill.
