# Ship a portfolio

Read first. Russian canon; if the user writes in English, open the same filename under `en/playbooks/`:

- Ship or Die, small bets — `playbooks/02-marc-lou.md`
- Volume, public building, cheap stack — `playbooks/03-pieter-levels.md`
- One channel per product, partnerships — `playbooks/07-tibo.md`

## Procedure

1. Cap work in progress. One product in public launch at a time.
2. Give each product one channel. Do not give every product the same X account job.
3. Ship ugly, measure money, then polish what converts.
4. Kill or pause bets that do not move in a fixed window. Do not keep a museum of MVPs.
5. Write the next 30 days as ship dates, not feature ideas.

## Do not

- Do not tell them to pick one company-for-life if they explicitly want a portfolio.
- Do not copy Pieter's lifestyle advice into a launch checklist. Use the shipping rules only.
